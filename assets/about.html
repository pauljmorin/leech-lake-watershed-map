About this map

Leech Lake Watershed

What this map is for

A map of the Leech Lake watershed — the whole Leech Lake River basin and the land that drains directly into the lake — with the lakes, streams, water accesses, water-quality stations, infested waters, reservation boundary and parcels inside it, over aerial photography going back to 1991. It is built to follow lakeshore and land-cover change across the watershed, year by year. Later it becomes a base for building other maps from.

How it is built

The map is a plain website — pages, a map, and data files in open formats — so it can be handed over and kept going without any special software. All data comes from public sources: the Minnesota DNR, the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency, the US Geological Survey, the Bureau of Indian Affairs, and the counties (Beltrami, Cass and Hubbard). The map holds no data of its own beyond those public records. Aerial imagery is streamed from the providers’ services (MnGeo and Esri Wayback) and is never downloaded onto this site’s server. The full list of every dataset, where it comes from, and how it was turned into what the map shows is in the Data sources document.

Privacy

The map shows only published public records. Parcel information is limited to published parcel facts (parcel number, acreage, plat and lot, use, and district information); no personal or financial fields are shown. The reservation boundary is the federal boundary as published, not a legal survey.

Who is behind it

The map is built and maintained by Paul Morin and Muse. Editing is Paul and Muse only for now; later, a couple of trusted users may get access for classification work.
